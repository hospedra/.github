# Hospreda

**A flexible, API-first property management platform for modern hospitality businesses.**

Hospreda is an extensible hotel management system designed for hotels, boutique hotels, motels, hostels, aparthotels, and other accommodation providers.

The platform aims to simplify daily property operations by bringing reservations, room inventory, guest profiles, front-desk workflows, housekeeping, pricing, payments, and reporting together in one modular system.

## Core Modules

* Property and organization management
* Room type and physical room inventory
* Availability calendar
* Reservation management
* Guest profiles and stay history
* Check-in and check-out workflows
* Room assignment and room changes
* Rate plans and daily pricing
* Folio, charges, deposits, and payments
* Housekeeping operations
* Role-based access control
* Audit logging
* Notifications and documents
* Operational and financial reporting

## Planned Integrations

* Online booking engine
* Channel managers
* Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, and other OTAs
* Payment gateways
* Electronic invoicing and accounting platforms
* Restaurant POS systems
* Door lock and access-control systems
* Revenue management platforms
* Guest communication services
* Webhooks and third-party applications

  
## Vision

Hospreda’s goal is to provide hospitality businesses with a modern, reliable, and integration-friendly alternative to rigid legacy property management systems.

> **Hospreda — Hospitality, orchestrated.**
